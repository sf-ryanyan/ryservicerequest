## Before You Submit / 提交之前

**If anyone is in danger, call 911 first.** If you smell gas, see fire or smoke, or a carbon monoxide alarm is going off, leave the home and call 911. Do not use this form to report an immediate danger.

**如果有人身安全危险，请先拨打 911。** 闻到燃气味、看到明火或烟雾、一氧化碳报警器响起时，请先离开房屋再拨 911。此类紧急情况不要通过本表单提交。

For a major water leak, flooding, sparks, or no power, fill in the form as usual and choose **Emergency** — those requests go to the top of the queue.

遇到大量漏水、积水、电火花或断电，请照常填写表单并选择 **Emergency**，这类申请会排在最前面处理。

## How to Submit a Request / 填写步骤

Five short sections. Fields marked with an asterisk are required; the rest are optional but genuinely help.

共五段。标星号为必填，其余选填，但填了确实有帮助。

**1. Your Contact Information / 联系方式**
Name, phone, and email, plus how you would rather be reached.
姓名、电话、邮箱，以及你希望被联系的方式。

**2. Property Address / 房屋地址**
Street, unit, city, state, and ZIP of the home that needs the repair.
需要维修的房屋的街道、Unit、城市、州和邮编。

**3. About the Issue / 问题详情**
Type of issue, which room, how urgent it is, and a description of at least 20 characters. When it started and whether it is still happening are optional, but they help a lot when judging the cause.
问题类型、哪个房间、紧急程度，以及至少 20 字的描述。开始时间和是否仍在发生为选填，但对判断原因很有帮助。

**4. Photos / 照片**
At least one, up to five. Photos are shrunk on your own phone before they are sent, so even large camera files upload quickly.
至少 1 张，最多 5 张。照片会先在你手机上压缩再上传，所以原图再大也传得快。

**5. Access & Scheduling / 进门与时间**
Whether a technician may enter while you are out, which times suit you, any pets, and a gate code or parking note.
你不在家时是否可以进门、哪些时段方便、家里有没有宠物，以及门禁密码或停车说明。

### Your progress is saved / 草稿会自动保存

Close the page part-way through and your answers come back the next time you open it. Photos are not saved and need to be added again.

填到一半关掉页面，下次打开会自动恢复之前填的内容。照片不会保存，需要重新上传。

## What Happens Next / 提交之后

1. A reference number appears on screen. Keep it — it identifies your request.
   页面会显示一个申请编号，请保留，后续沟通以此为准。

2. A confirmation email is sent to the address you entered.
   确认邮件会发送到你填写的邮箱。

3. Requests are handled by urgency — Emergency first, then Urgent, then Routine — and you will be contacted to agree on a time.
   申请按紧急程度处理：Emergency → Urgent → Routine，我方会联系你商定时间。

4. No reply within 24 hours? Just reply to your confirmation email; it reaches us directly.
   24 小时内没有回复？直接回复那封确认邮件即可联系到我们。

## Tips for a Faster Repair / 让维修更快的几个建议

- Take one close photo of the problem and one wider shot of the room around it.
  拍一张问题特写，再拍一张房间全景。

- Be specific about the location. "Under the kitchen sink, left side" beats "kitchen".
  位置写具体：「厨房水槽下方左侧」比「厨房」有用得多。

- Note anything the technician needs in order to get in: gate code, where to park, a dog that will be home.
  师傅进门需要知道的都写上：门禁密码、停车位置、家里有狗。

- Choosing **Yes, you may enter** usually gets the repair finished days sooner.
  选择「可以进门」通常能让维修提前好几天完成。

- Mention what you already tried, such as shutting a valve or resetting a breaker.
  已经试过的处理也写上，比如关了阀门、重置了跳闸开关。
